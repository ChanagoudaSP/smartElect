Help Center

Find answers to common questions about using Smart-Elect

Voting Guide
  • How to Vote:

    Open an active election, select your preferred candidate, and confirm your vote before the deadline.

  • Vote Security:

    Your vote is encrypted and anonymous. Only verified users can cast a ballot in an active election.

  • View Results:

    Once an election ends, you can view the results from your dashboard.

Account Security
  • Account Security:

    Your account uses a secure password and verified identity checks to protect your access.

  • Password Safety:

    Use a strong password and never share it with anyone.

  • Account Recovery:

    Contact the administrators if you need to recover your account.

Common Issues
  • Login Issues:

    Verify that your email and password are correct, and contact the admin if your account is locked.

  • Cannot Vote:

    Make sure the election is active and you haven't already voted.

  • Technical Problems:

    Clear your browser cache or try a different browser if you experience issues.

Need More Help?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question, please contact the system administrators for assistance.
